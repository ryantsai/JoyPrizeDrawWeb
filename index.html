Questions

What people ask before going live.

Can I draw prizes, people, or both?

Both. Use it for item-only draws, or turn on Participant mode to pair prizes with a separate list of people.

How do I stop the same winner from appearing twice?

Use the remove-winner option for items, or set win limits in Participant mode when the same person should not be selected again.

Can I prepare lists before an event starts?

Yes. Type items manually, paste one-per-line lists, auto-generate numbered entries, and save reusable packs for later.

Will my packs and history stay on this device?

Yes. The app stores its working data locally on the device unless you explicitly export or share it.

Can I review or export results afterward?

Yes. Winner history stays available in the app, and you can export data as CSV or TXT.
